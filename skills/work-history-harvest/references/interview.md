# Interview

Fill the gaps in the document by asking the person, and record the answers as clean records. Asking needs no disclosures: the person's answers are not a source the harvest opens. When an answer points to a document or a work system, read it through [harvest](harvest.md) instead.

1. **Collect the open questions.** Read the records and the "Open questions" section of the document, and add to that list:
   - the questions under [Ask once](#ask-once) that the records and the About section do not answer yet;
   - questions the person brings from elsewhere, such as the "no evidence yet" terms of a LinkedIn audit or the requirements of a job posting: for each, whether the person has used the skill or done the work, and if so where and how. "No" is a fine answer; only what has real work behind it becomes a record.

   When the document has no records at all, for instance because the person has no access to work systems and no documents, take the [guided path](#guided-path) as well.

   Done when the open list holds every gap and every question above that is still open, each once.

2. **Ask a round** of at most five questions, the most valuable first:
   - results and numbers ("Roughly how much faster? A range is fine.");
   - the person's own part (led, built, contributed, supported);
   - scale of the person's own work: team size, how much they handled, how often (the employer's business figures, such as revenue, client counts or transaction volumes, stay out);
   - what only the person knows: effect on users or the business, work across teams, things they started without being asked, mentoring, hiring, onboarding.

   Frame each question around the challenge, what the person did, and what changed. Every question can be answered "skip". Questions marked "(before access ends)" come first while the person still has access.

   Done when the person has answered, skipped or deferred each question of the round.

3. **Write each answer** into the record it belongs to, or a new record, with Basis `self-reported` or `mixed` and status `pending`. Before an answer changes an approved record, copy its approved text as [Owner review](../SKILL.md#owner-review) says. Clean the answer before writing it: when it names a client, a colleague or an internal system, or gives an employer's business figure, generalize it and tell the person what you changed. Write the record in the document's style, in full sentences, even when the answer came in a few words. A title answer goes into the Titles line and gets its own title section ([document format](document-format.md)). Work that belongs to no employer, such as a course during a career break, goes into `work-history-independent.md`, created from the template like any other document.

   Done when every answer of the round that adds or changes evidence sits in a clean record.

4. **Update the open list.** Remove answered and skipped questions from "Open questions", and mark deferred ones "(deferred)" so a later round asks them again.

   Done when the list holds only the questions still open.

5. **Repeat** until no open questions remain or the person stops. Then run the NDA pass from the [confidentiality procedure](confidentiality.md) over the changed records, review them with the person as in [harvest](harvest.md) step 8, scan again after their edits and restores, and finish as [End of every run](../SKILL.md#end-of-every-run) says, with a Harvest log row of run `interview`.

   Done when the NDA pass is clean, the person has approved, edited or dropped every changed record, and the About section is current.

## Ask once

Ask these once each, in either path:

- **Titles.** Every title the person held at the employer, as it was officially given, with the month and year each began, promotions and role changes included. When a title is internal or unusual, also ask what the role did and at what level.
- **After the job,** once it has ended: what the person is doing in the meantime, such as courses, certificates, projects, freelance or interim work, volunteering. Record each in `work-history-independent.md`, or freelance work in a document for the person's own business, with the skills it shows. Ask whether the person wants the end of the job recorded in the About section, and record only the plain fact, such as "left to take a career break". Details of how the job ended that concern other people or the employer stay out unless the employer has made them public.
- **AI tools.** Which AI tools or apps the person used regularly, and for what. Add their public names to the Technologies of the records where the person used them.

## Guided path

When the person has their LinkedIn positions (the Positions file of their LinkedIn data download, or the profile saved as PDF) or an old CV, start from the titles and dates listed there; open only that file, after the disclosures of [harvest](harvest.md) step 1. Start with the titles from [Ask once](#ask-once).

For each title, newest first:

- What was the role about, in one sentence? How big was the team, and which teams did you work with?
- Which two to four pieces of work are you proudest of? For each: the challenge, what you did, what changed, and a number.
- What did you start or improve without being asked?
- What did your manager or colleagues praise you for? Themes only, with who praised it by relationship, such as manager or peer.
- Which tools and technologies did you use every week?

Then, once: certificates and courses, public work (talks, articles, open source, side projects) and volunteering from this job's period.

Done when every open question is answered, skipped or deferred by the person, every answer that adds or changes evidence sits in the document as a clean record, and the person has approved the changed records.
