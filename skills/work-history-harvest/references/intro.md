# First-run intro

Your first reply on a first run opens with this text, in the person's language, keeping its headings, bullets and bold. Translate it plainly; leave out nothing and add nothing but the questions of [setup](setup.md) and, when access ends within days, the list of what to save first that setup describes. On later runs, open with the [reminder](#reminder) instead.

## Intro

**What this is**
I collect your work at one employer into one document you keep: what you did, how, and what came of it. Your LinkedIn profile, your CV and your interview answers can come from it later.

**What I can use** (all optional)
- **Work systems:** Jira, Confluence, GitLab or GitHub.
- **Documents:** an old CV, performance reviews (PDF or screenshots), certificates.
- **Your Claude history:** Claude Code on this computer, or the claude.ai data export.
- **Names that must never appear:** clients, colleagues, internal systems.

**What I never save**
Client and colleague names, code, ticket numbers, internal systems, company figures. Only your own actions and results, in general words.

**Your NDA**
You can give me your NDA, the confidentiality part of your contract or your exit agreement. Exit agreements often add rules, such as keeping the agreement secret or not speaking badly of the company. Give only the clauses I need, without salary or personal data. Before you upload, check your company's AI policy on a work account, or whether your chats train models on a personal one.

**You are responsible for the document**
I make mistakes. Read all of it and check it against your contract and NDA before you copy it off a work computer. This is not legal advice.

**Where to run me**
- **Claude Code:** files stay on your computer, scripts run, GitLab and GitHub work through their command-line tools.
- **claude.ai:** easier if you have never used a terminal. You download the work as a ZIP after each step; Jira and Confluence work through connectors.
- Your claude.ai chats reach both through the claude.ai data export, unless an admin turned it off on a work account.

**Good to know**
- I work best on the strongest model available.
- A few documents take about an hour, years of Jira and GitLab a few hours over several sittings. You can stop at any time and continue later.
