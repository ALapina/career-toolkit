The list holds the paths of the files the run created. Every path in it that lies in work-history/ but outside work-history/local-only/ is work-history/progress.md or a work history document named work-history-<employer>.md, such as work-history/work-history-contoso-logistics.md or work-history/work-history-northwind-traders.md. Paths in work-history/local-only/ and paths outside work-history/ do not count.
